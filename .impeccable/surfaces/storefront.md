# Storefront
Mode: Persuade with shopping functionality.

## Direction contract
THESIS: A neighborhood bottle shop with the confidence of a considered editorial campaign. The supplied grape logo and all eight shopping categories remain; the craft pass replaces repetitive pacing with distinct campaign, shelf, and neighborhood moments.
OWN-WORLD: Established oxblood #641b2e, paper #fbfaf7, aged gold #b99a62. Bodoni Moda headlines and Geist shopping controls. Real contained product photographs, a new burgundy editorial campaign photograph, fine rules, generous section spacing, measured quiet press feedback.
STORY: Adults verify 21+, recognize the store, find wine/beer/spirits through search or familiar categories, explore four curated sample collections, and find the newsletter. No invented discounts, stock or live integration.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Compact logo/search/bag header and eight category links; inset full-width burgundy still-life with cream headline at left and glasses/bottle at right. Slider has explicit arrows, pause, selectors and touch swipe. Red/white panels use legible side-by-side imagery; mobile panels become compact full-width rows. Mobile home product shelves scroll natively with a visible next-card cue; shop results retain a two-column grid.
FORM: A substantial refinement of the user-pinned burgundy-and-gold retail world, using Emil design-engineering, animate and mobile-native skills. The user authorizes all necessary improvements. No new brand or competing visual-world selection. Code-led, no UI comp. Signature motion is brief spatial bag entry/exit plus persistent toast feedback; keyboard and reduced-motion paths stay immediate. Native browser focus and scrolling remain intact.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
